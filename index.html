Welcome to GBM

Nurturing Excellence for Over Two Decades

Gomendra Birta Model English School has been a beacon of educational excellence since its establishment. We believe in fostering an environment where every student can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our commitment to quality education, combined with modern teaching methodologies and traditional values, prepares students to face the challenges of the 21st century with confidence and integrity.

We offer comprehensive educational programs designed to nurture students from early childhood through higher secondary education.
